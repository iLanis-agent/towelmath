Towel math

The label says 500 GSM, the machine says 7 kg, and nobody knows how many bath towels that actually is. Towel math turns size and GSM into honest grams, grams into towels per load and loads per pile, and the weekly habit into loads and cost per year. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Weight plan - size and GSM become the towel's weight in grams.
  • Load plan - weight, count and machine size become towels per load and loads per pile.
  • Cost plan - loads a week become loads and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled GSM: about 350-450 for everyday towels, 500-700 for the heavy ones. Labeled machine: 7-9 kg for a household washer. Labeled load cost: about 0.9 a load for water and energy. These are reference values, not rules - the towel's label and your machine's manual carry the real numbers.
